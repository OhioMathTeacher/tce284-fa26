Week 10 · Monday — How a Currere Closes

Act II · currere · mentors · endings · your close · work time
Today's goals: Hear what Romano says about learning from writers you admire, read four currere endings in groups of three, gush a possible ending of your own, then work on your draft with your group.

1 · Learning from mentors (WWM ch19) (~10 min) · slides (private link)

  • A few passages from WWM ch19, "Mentors, Imitation, Apprenticeship," on the slides.
  • Open ch19 in Journaler's Readings as we go. Mark a passage and type what you would borrow.

Purpose: Romano learned from writers he never met. Today the mentors are four currere writers, and the move you borrow is an ending.

2 · How a currere closes, in threes (~20 min)

  • Count off into groups of three. Each of you takes one ending from the slides:
    • 1 · Daspit, "None of Us"
    • 2 · Romano, "The Anchor, the Bridge, the Lesson"
    • 3 · Bird, "Self-Coaching Emotions"
  • Read yours, then tell your group what your writer does on the last page.
  • Then, as a whole class, listen to me read the fourth ending: Edwards, "Who's Cheating Whom?"

Purpose: each of you reads one ending closely, your group hears three, and the whole class hears the fourth read aloud.

3 · Gush a possible ending (~10 min)

  • In Journaler, Freewrite/OPs → Open page. Set the timer to 5:00 and go. Don't stop, don't fix. When it ends, press + Add to notebook.
  • Your Writer's Notebook is due Wednesday, December 2, and it lives in Journaler. Open My Progress to see where you stand. If you haven't opened Journaler in a while and saved a backup of your work, you really should: Save my work.

Purpose: get a first try at your close on the page, and keep the notebook moving.

4 · Work time, with your group (~40 min)

  • Read each other your endings. Talk. Then keep going on your draft, starting with what your conference named.
  • I'll circulate. Grab me for the one thing you're stuck on.

Purpose: the longest block of the day goes to your own draft, with your group in reach.

Wrap-up & before Wednesday:
  • Keep revising toward your Final Draft (due Mon Nov 2, 11:59 p.m.). Bring a version you'd be willing to read a piece of aloud.
  • Wednesday we record the audio for your author page and go hunting for the genres buried in your currere — the start of Act III.
