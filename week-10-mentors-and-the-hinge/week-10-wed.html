Week 10 · Wednesday — Your Voice, and the Genres Buried Inside

Act II finale · author audio · the hinge to Act III
Last day of Act II. You record your currere in your own voice, then find the many genres hiding inside it — the seeds of the multigenre project you begin next.
Today's goals: Record the audio for your author page, then comb your currere for the genres buried inside it — a letter, a list, a map — the start of Act III.

0 · Daily Poem — "Song of Myself, 51" (~10 min)

  • Walt Whitman, "Song of Myself, 51". Read it aloud — it was built to be. "Do I contradict myself? / Very well then I contradict myself, / (I am large, I contain multitudes.)" Your one currere is one voice — but how many kinds of writing are packed inside it?

Purpose: a poem meant for the voice, on the day you record yours — and its "I contain multitudes" is the exact hinge into the buried genres.

1 · Record your author-page audio (~25 min)

  • Choose a passage of your currere you'd want heard in your own voice — the close, or the moment you're proudest of. Read it a couple of times, then record it for your author page.
  • Read it like you mean it, not like a report — this is the Voice Print, out loud. Find a quiet corner; record in Journaler or on your phone, whichever you like. You'll submit the recording with your Final Draft.

Purpose: the currere becomes a spoken thing, in the writer's actual voice — the un-fakeable center of the whole course, heard.

2 · Hunt the buried genres (~30 min)

  • Your currere is one form — but buried inside it are many. Comb your draft moment by moment and ask: what genre is hiding here?
  • A letter you never sent. A list. A recipe. A map. A text thread. A eulogy. A set of instructions. A field guide. A report card. Write down every genre your moments could become.
  • Star the two or three that pull hardest — the ones you'd actually want to write. Those are your opening palette for Act III.

Purpose: surface the genres already latent in the thread, so the multigenre project grows out of the currere instead of starting from scratch.

3 · Act III at a glance (~10 min)

  • A first look at where we're headed: the multigenre project takes your one thread and tells it outward, across several of the genres you just found.
  • Nothing due yet — just know the genres you starred today are where it begins. We pick this up next week.

Purpose: close Act II by opening the door to Act III — the thread you found is about to go many places.

Wrap-up & at home:
  • Finish your currere Final Draft and submit it with your author-page audio. (Due Fri Oct 30 · Canvas.)
  • Keep the buried-genres list. It's the first thing we reach for in Act III.
