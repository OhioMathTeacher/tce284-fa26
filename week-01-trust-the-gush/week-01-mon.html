Week 1 · Monday — Welcome

Act I · what matters to you, and what can only you write?
Today's goals: Meet each other and the course. Start where the whole semester starts — with something that matters to you — and meet Journaler, the app we write in all term.

0 · Housekeeping (~15 min)

  • Name badges and markers around.
  • Open Journaler, the app we write in all term — bookmark it.
  • In Journaler, open Settings → AI and choose Groq. Follow Get a key, then paste the key back into Settings. The key stays in your browser and goes only to Groq. We do this together.
  • Download Write What Matters (Romano) Intro · ch1 · ch2 from Canvas Files, then load them into Journaler's Readings tab.
  • Take two minutes to page through ch1. Jot down 3–4 words for features that jump out at you. Be ready to share one.

Purpose: settle in, get the tools working before we need them, and start noticing before we start talking.

1 · Introductions — draw what matters to you (~50 min)

  • Brief syllabus overview (~10). The course's question, plainly: when a machine can generate any kind of writing, what is left that only you can do? We start with what only you can — what matters to you. And the shape of the term, in three acts: Act I — Become a Writer (five short pieces) → Act II — The Currere (one deep thread across your life in schools) → Act III — a Multimodal Research Project (your work, turned outward) — each one changing how you work with, and against, the machine.
  • Make it (~25): draw an issue or topic that matters to you. With the drawing, write a paragraph on what it shows and why it matters to you.
  • Share (~15): introduce yourself, your drawing, and why it matters.

Purpose: meet each other through real material, and put the course's premise in the room on day one — your own subject, in your own hand.

2 · Free writing — your first write in Journaler (~15 min)

  • Quick talk: what is free writing, what is it good for, why do it?
  • Your first write, in the app: start a 5-minute timer and go — why do we write? Gush mode locks editing, so don't stop and don't fix. When the timer ends, answer the few questions the app asks. This one is just for you; you'll come back to it later.

Purpose: meet the app and the timer on day one, and put down a first page you'll return to.

Wrap-up & before Wednesday:
  • Read Edwards, "Who's Cheating Whom?" (Currere Exchange Journal) plus Write What Matters, Intro · ch1 · ch2 (pp. xi–8, in Canvas Files).
  • Do this reading in Journaler. The Write What Matters chapters are already in your Readings tab from today — download the Edwards PDF from the link above and load it in beside them, so both readings live in one place.
  • As you read, drag a box around what strikes you and choose ✎ Highlight, then 📓 Keep in notebook — at least three passages across the two readings. Reading in Journaler is note-taking, and nothing you write there leaves your computer. Keeping a passage files it with its page number — and it becomes a notebook entry when you write what you make of it. My Progress lists each reading that is waiting on you, and you write there.
  • Keep one dated notebook entry before Wednesday: what do these two readings disagree about? Both are about writing and machines, and they do not want the same thing from you.
  • Read the syllabus; bring questions Wednesday.
