Week 11 · Wednesday — Topic, Map & Sources

Act III · Multimodal Research Project · talk it through · map your top 2 · the three rings · the proposal
Today's goals: Choose your topic, map it, sort its sources into three rings, set up your Gemini Notebook, and start your proposal.

1 · Talk through your topics (~10 min)
  • With a partner, share your list of five possible topics and your top 2 from Monday.

Purpose: say your topics out loud before you map them.

2 · Topic Map Studio (~25 min)
  • A quick look at the Topic Map (5 min)
  • Map your top 2 (20 min)
    • Fill a Topic Map for each of your top 2 ideas. Whichever one fills in more easily is your topic.

3 · The three source rings (~10 min)
  • I'll walk the three rings with one example: witnesses (people who know it firsthand — the interviews you conduct), traces (the documentary record — obituary, article, lyrics, label, letter), context (the background world — the history of the place and era).
  • You: on your Topic Map, sort your topic's likely sources into the three rings.

Purpose: a project built only from the outer ring reads as generic — make sure you have real witnesses and traces, not just context.

4 · Plan your primary source & set up Gemini Notebook (~20 min)
  • You: name the one primary source you'll gather yourself — the interview you'll conduct, the place you'll observe, the artifact you'll collect. Draft the ask or the plan: who, and when. This is on a real clock — line it up today.
  • Then open Gemini Notebook, create your notebook, and add the sources you already have. I'll demo the setup and circulate — it answers only from what you give it, so its help is only as good as your pile.

Purpose: schedule the fieldwork before anything else can crowd it out, and stand up the research home you'll use all month.

5 · Start your proposal (~10 min)
  • Draft your proposal from your Topic Map, using the Proposal guidelines you read for homework.
  • I'll circulate and sign off on topics as you go.
Wrap-up & before Monday (5 min):
  • Finish and submit the proposal (due Sun Nov 8, 11:59 p.m.). Keep gathering — and send that interview request.
  • Monday: we interrogate your sources in Gemini Notebook, watch the machine go generic, and start your first genres. Draft due Fri Nov 20.
