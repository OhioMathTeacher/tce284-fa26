Week 11 · Wednesday — Sources & Setup

Act III · Multimodal Research Project · the three rings · plan the interview · the proposal
Today's goals: Lock your topic, plan the one source you have to gather yourself, stand up your NotebookLM notebook, and draft your proposal.

1 · The three source rings (~15 min)

  • I'll walk the three rings with one example: witnesses (people who know it firsthand — the interviews you conduct), traces (the documentary record — obituary, article, lyrics, label, letter), context (the background world — the history of the place and era).
  • You: on your Topic Map, sort your topic's likely sources into the three rings.

Purpose: a project built only from the outer ring reads as generic — make sure you have real witnesses and traces, not just context.

2 · Plan your primary source & set up NotebookLM (~25 min)

  • You: name the one primary source you'll gather yourself — the interview you'll conduct, the place you'll observe, the artifact you'll collect. Draft the ask or the plan: who, and when. This is on a real clock — line it up today.
  • Then open NotebookLM, create your notebook, and add the sources you already have. I'll demo the setup and circulate — it answers only from what you give it, so its help is only as good as your pile.

Purpose: schedule the fieldwork before anything else can crowd it out, and stand up the research home you'll use all month.

3 · Proposal work time (~40 min)

  • You: draft your proposal from your Topic Map — (a) topic; (b) what you know; (c) what you want to learn; (d) research questions; (e) research plan; (f) a preliminary source list naming a primary source you can gather, a peer-reviewed article, and a long-form work.
  • I'll circulate and sign off on topics as you go.

Purpose: a proposal that commits to a real primary source and a doable plan — the spine of everything that follows.

Wrap-up & before Monday:
  • Finish and submit the proposal (due Fri Nov 6). Keep gathering — and send that interview request.
  • Monday: we interrogate your sources in NotebookLM, watch the machine go generic, and start your first genres. Draft due Fri Nov 20.
