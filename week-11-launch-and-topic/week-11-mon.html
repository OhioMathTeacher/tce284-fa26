Week 11 · Monday — Launching the Research Project

Act III · Multimodal Research Project · talk back to the machine · find your thread
Today's goals: Launch the Multimodal Research Project: meet the machine by talking back to it, start your Topic Map, and find the thread you'll chase for the next month.

1 · Talk back to the machine (~20 min)

  • Listen: a short AI-generated audio overview of this very assignment, built from the project guidelines.
  • Then, together — three questions:
    • Where did it go generic? Find a sentence that could describe almost any assignment.
    • What did it flatten or skip?
    • What did it get right?
  • Name what just happened: someone played an AI overview and argued with it. That's this whole project in miniature — the machine hands you the average; your job is the particular.

Purpose: the recursive opener — the first thing you do in Act III is talk back to a machine, using the assignment itself, so the project's central move is felt before it's explained.

2 · What this project is (~15 min)

  • Walk the project guidelines and how it's scored. Where the currere turned inward, this turns outward — onto a subject in the world — and asks you to render your research across many forms.
  • The pieces, quickly: the eight required genres · at least one transformed AI artifact · a reflective note. The sources: a primary source you gather yourself, a peer-reviewed article, and a long-form work.

Purpose: see the whole shape and how it's judged before you commit to a topic — you just heard the machine's version; here's the real one.

3 · Topic Map studio — fill the wall (~35 min)

  • Open the Topic Brainstorming Worksheet. Every topic is made of seven facets — People · Moments · Places · Objects · Words · Questions · Numbers. Under each, list everything you can. Go for quantity; don't judge yet.
  • Pair up. Share your fullest facet; your partner adds three things you missed.
  • Circle the two or three items you can't stop thinking about. That's your thread.

Purpose: a topic you can both research and render, found by filling a wall rather than staring at a blank page — and a first test of whether there's enough there to sustain a month.

Wrap-up & before Wednesday:
  • Keep filling your Topic Map. Start hunting sources — and think hard about who you could interview or what you could go see (that one takes real-world time).
  • Wednesday: we plan your primary source and set up your NotebookLM notebook. The proposal is due Fri Nov 6.
