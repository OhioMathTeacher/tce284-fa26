Week 11 · Monday — Launching the Research Project

Act III · Multimodal Research Project · talk back to the machine · find your thread
Today's goals: Launch the Multimodal Research Project: meet the machine by talking back to it, look closely at a finished project with a partner, and brainstorm possible topics.

1 · Introduction to the project (~25 min)
  • Skim the project guidelines (5 min)
    • The pieces: the eight required pieces · at least four more genres of your choosing · one audio and one video piece, 1 to 3 minutes each, made by you · at least one transformed AI artifact · a reflective note.
    • The sources: a primary source you gather yourself, a peer-reviewed article, and a long-form work.
    • See finished ones: sample multigenre projects.
  • Listen and mark (10 min)
    • A podcast about this assignment, made by AI from the guidelines you are holding. (5:03)
    • Mark the guidelines as you listen. Note what the hosts say that is on the page, what they get wrong, and what they never mention.
  • Talk back to the machine (5 min)
    • Together, three questions:
      • Where did it go generic? Find a sentence that could describe almost any assignment.
      • What did it flatten or skip?
      • What did it get right?
    • Then ask the notebook your own questions about the assignment, and check each answer against the guidelines.
    • Name what just happened: we played an AI overview of the assignment and argued with it. That is this project in small. The machine gives you the general version of any topic. Your job is the specific.
    • Before we move on: what the podcast got right, and what it missed.
  • Quick quiz: does it count? (5 min)

Purpose: read the project in its own words first, then hear a machine's version of it and check one against the other.

2 · Sample multigenre projects (~25 min)
  • With a partner, read one sample (10 min)
    • Pick one project from the shelf of sample multigenre projects. The two AI editions count too. One pair per project.
    • As you read, note the genres it uses and what ties them together.
  • Join another pair (10 min)
    • Tell each other about your projects.
  • Class debrief (5 min)

Purpose: see what a finished project can look like before you brainstorm your own.

3 · Topic brainstorm (~25 min)
  • Gush five possible topics (10 min)
  • Share with a partner (10 min)
    • Talk through your lists. From those conversations, pick your top 2.
  • Class debrief (5 min)

Purpose: have a few real topics on paper before you choose one. Wednesday you map your top 2.

Wrap-up & before Wednesday (5 min):
  • Keep working on your topic brainstorm list. Bring it to class on Wednesday.
  • Wednesday we hone your topic and fill in a Topic Map for your top 2 ideas.
  • Read the Proposal guidelines before Wednesday. The proposal is due Sun Nov 8, 11:59 p.m.
