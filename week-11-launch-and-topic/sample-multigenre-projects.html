Act III · Multimodal Research Project

Sample Multigenre Projects

Each title opens the PDF in our Canvas course.

  1. 01

    The Allosaurs in Phoenix

    Dinosaurs

    A science-fiction story of allosaurs loose in Phoenix, told in a diary, a newspaper article, a two-voice poem, a cartoon, and an encyclopedia entry. The writer says which parts are true.

  2. 02

    Blood and Bravery

    Bram Stoker's Dracula

    Retells Dracula through maps, poems, and Jonathan Harker's travels, and gives the Count the voice the novel never does. The novel is itself told in letters and diaries.

  3. 03

    Call to Change

    Racial profiling and policing

    Song lyrics, protest poems, a definition, and federal crime statistics ask whether racial profiling is a serious problem in American law enforcement.

  4. 04

    Calumet Country Club: New Members Packet

    Working at a country club

    Built as a welcome packet for new members: the manager's letter, a waitress's unofficial glossary of terms, and poems about golf, all from the staff side of the club.

  5. 05

    A Columbine Connection

    The Columbine shooting

    Written the summer after the 1999 shooting. A teacher at her students' funerals, a shooter's inner voice, and a poem about waiting by the TV ask questions that have no answers.

  6. 06

    Cracking the Egg

    The egg

    Starts with childhood breakfasts, then follows the egg as a creation myth, a word (“egged on”), a hidden message, and the first step of a controversial science.

  7. 07

    Frozen Mourning

    The Titanic

    Asks what flaws doomed a ship that could have been saved. A newspaper scene, a timeline, the disaster in numbers, and poems put you on board.

  8. 08

    Giving a Voice to Women - with Long or Short Hair

    Hurston's Their Eyes Were Watching God

    The writer reads Janie's search for her own voice beside her own, and stages Hurston's critics, including an imagined interview with Richard Wright.

  9. 09

    Growing Up Spanglish

    Spanglish and Latino identity

    Treats Spanglish as more than mixed language: a way of living between two worlds. History, statistics, and definitions follow one girl's search for where she belongs.

  10. 10

    Inside Out - A Taming of Disorderly Anxiety

    Anxiety disorders

    Anxiety from the inside out: first what it feels like, in metaphor and dialogue, then the options and consequences of treatment.

  11. 11

    Long Strange Trip: The Interstate Highway System and the American Imagination

    The Interstate Highway System

    A children's-book welcome to the interstates, the numbers behind them, and voices from the road, like a long-haul trucker's.

  12. 12

    The Lunchroom

    The high school cafeteria

    Four years in one room: from the new girl with no one to sit with to the cafeteria as the stage for high school's social wars.

  13. 13

    The Man Behind the Movies: The Life and Career of Steven Speilberg

    Steven Spielberg

    Spielberg's life and films, opening with a young boy watching Jurassic Park for the first time on a family road trip.

  14. 14

    Marilyn

    Marilyn Monroe

    Looks past the screen goddess to the woman, through her own quotes and pieces on her highs and lows.

  15. 15

    May the Roads Rise to Meet You

    Addiction and grief

    Opens as a fairy tale about a girl whose father has a disease she does not yet understand, then turns to warning labels and other genres about addiction, relapse, and loss.

  16. 16

    Opening Closed Doors

    Adoption

    The real and imagined story of the writer's own adoption, told from several sides: the birth mother, the parents who waited years, and the child.

  17. 17

    They Say She Ain't No Hero

    Wonder Woman

    Asks why girls don't say they want to be Wonder Woman, retells her origin in verse, and looks at her creator's hopes and the sexism she met.

  18. 18

    Third Culture Kid

    Growing up abroad

    Life as a kid who moved from Illinois to Luxembourg at six: lists, letters, a map of the people she loves, and a seven-part story about coming home.

  19. 19

    Viva Viola

    The viola

    The viola introduces itself in a Dear Reader letter, then an epic tells its rise from forgotten instrument to solo voice. Meant to be read with a soundtrack of two viola concertos.

  20. 20

    We Matter: Black Lives in Today's America

    Black Lives Matter

    Opens with a letter telling every reader they matter, then an obituary for Trayvon Martin, a birth certificate for Black Lives Matter, and more.

  21. 21

    When Life Hands You Lemons

    Wolff's Make Lemonade

    A novel of poverty, young motherhood, and a fourteen-year-old babysitter saving for college, explored through poems and other genres.

  22. 22

    The Wizard of Oz

    The making of the 1939 film

    Ten screenwriters, four directors, and a film that lost money, plus a letter from an imagined studio hand whose name is in no book.
