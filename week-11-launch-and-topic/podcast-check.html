The Podcast, Checked

Act III · Multimodal Research Project · what it got right · what it missed

The podcast was made by AI from the project guidelines. The guidelines are the assignment. Where the two disagree, the guidelines win.

What it got right
  • Twelve or more pieces, turned in as one PDF.
  • Eight of the twelve are required, and one of them is poetry in contemporary free verse.
  • Unifying elements hold the pieces together: a repetend, repeated images, or a fragmented narrative.
  • Audio and video pieces, 1 to 3 minutes each, are linked from inside the PDF.
  • An AI-generated artifact is required, and it has to be transformed. Raw output earns nothing in that row.
  • A podcast made from a journal article is not your piece.
  • The Required pieces row (10 points) checks only that each piece is there. Quality is judged under Holistic impact (10 points).
  • The reflection on AI cannot be a general statement about AI.

What it missed

What it got wrong or stretched

  • "Your grade relies entirely on the detail that only your unique sources hold." That describes one row, Holistic impact, which is 10 of the 35 points.
  • "Submitting raw output earns zero points." Raw output cannot be your transformed AI artifact. It can still appear in your project, labeled as AI-made, but it never counts as one of your pieces.
  • The reflection means you "confess where the AI flattened your work" and what you changed. The guidelines ask for more: "where AI helped, where it flattened, what you changed and why."
  • "If you input your own original lyrics, that song counts as your piece." And: "If I use Notebook LM to summarize my own personal journal entries, it is mine." What AI makes is others' work, even when it is made from yours. The song and the podcast do not count. Your lyric sheet and your journal entries do.

What it never mentioned

  • Sources. At least one primary source you gather yourself (an interview, a firsthand observation, an artifact), one peer-reviewed journal article, and one long-form work. Your primary source takes real-world time, so start now.
  • Six of the eight required pieces. The Preface or Dear Reader, the informational essay (250 to 350 words), a visual element of your own, note pages, Works Cited, and the Finale.
  • Labels. Label every piece with its genre. Label every AI-generated work as AI-made and say what you did with it.
  • A table of contents listing every piece and where it lives.
  • Your work and others' work. Work made by another person or by AI can appear in your project, but it never counts as one of your pieces. Inside one of your pieces, cite it and keep it under about a tenth of the piece.
  • Your AI artifact can be critical. A piece that exposes what the machine gets wrong counts as fully as one that puts AI to work.
  • The due date. One PDF via Canvas by Wednesday, December 9 at 11:59 p.m.
