Telephone, except the machine is every player. Pass zero is your Breaking the Rules page. Each time you press AI Revise, the machine gets the latest pass and whatever requests are ticked, and what comes back pops up as the next tab. Nothing it says is written back into your page.

Read the strip. The percentage under each pass is how many of your pass-zero words are still on the page. Each pass is fed the one before it, the way a whisper is passed down a line. Watch what goes first, and what refuses to go.
