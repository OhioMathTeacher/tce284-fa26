Week 2 · Wednesday — Build the page

Act I · Writing Place · what does your place look, sound, and feel like?
Today's goals: Loosen up with Lamott, share your writing-place photos, sharpen the words with imagery, and compose OP2 — words and photos on one page. Due Friday.

0 · Daily Poem — "I dwell in Possibility" (~10 min)

  • Emily Dickinson, "I dwell in Possibility –". Read it aloud. She calls poetry a house — "A fairer House than Prose – / More numerous of Windows –." What kind of house is your writing?

Purpose: the standing ritual — the writer's place as a dwelling, on the day you build your own.

1 · Housekeeping (~5 min)

  • Questions on OP2. Everyone bring their photos?

2 · Anne Lamott on writing (~10 min)

  • Watch Anne Lamott's writing tips. As you watch, jot one thing she says about how drafting really works.
  • Quick talk: what permission is she giving you for the page you're about to build? (Her "let it be bad" comes back in Week 5.)

Purpose: loosen the grip before composing — a real writer saying the first pass is supposed to be rough.

3 · Share your writing-place photos (~15 min)

  • In small groups, then a few to the room: show your photos and say one thing your place needs. What surprised you about seeing yourself at work?

Purpose: seeing others' places sharpens your own — and puts real images in front of you before you write around them.

4 · Imagery — make us see it (~15 min)

  • Quick mini-lesson: concrete, sensory words beat general ones. Not "a nice quiet spot" — the hum of the mini-fridge, the coffee ring on the desk, the chair that tilts.
  • Quick-write: describe your writing place in five sensory details — one each for sight, sound, smell, touch, and the time of day.

Purpose: give the One-Pager specific language, and plant the senses — the move OP3 will build on.

5 · Compose OP2 in Journaler (~20 min)

  • Build your Writing Place One-Pager: your observations + 2–3 photos (one of you at work), one page. Pull the strongest lines from your gush and your imagery quick-write.
  • Trade with a neighbor for one read: what do they see clearly, and where do they want a sharper detail?

Purpose: get most of the page down in the room, with a reader, so the take-home is polish, not a blank start.

Wrap-up & at home:
  • Finish OP2 — Writing Place and export a one-page PDF. (Due Fri · Canvas.)
  • Read Daspit, "'None of Us'" + listen to Romano reading "My Father's Voice" + WWM ch9–10 (Hearkening to Voices; Dialog) for Wed Sep 9 — no class Labor Day Monday.
