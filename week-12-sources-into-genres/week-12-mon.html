Week 12 · Monday — Interrogate & Draft

Act III · Multimodal Research Project · the NotebookLM lab · watch it flatten · first genres
Today's goals: Use your grounded notebook to find what your sources actually say, see the machine flatten your topic to the average, and draft your first two required genres.

1 · NotebookLM lab (~20 min)

  • You: add any new sources, then ask your notebook real questions about your topic — what surprises you, what your sources disagree about, what a claim actually rests on. Save the useful bits.
  • I'll show one interrogation on screen and circulate.

Purpose: the grounded machine is only as good as your pile — use it to mine what you've already gathered.

2 · The flattening demo (~15 min)

  • I'll ask a plain chatbot to "write about" a topic and we'll read the result together — accurate, smooth, and generic.
  • You: do the same with your topic. Save that generic version. It's your "before."

Purpose: feel the average so you can beat it — the gap between the machine's version and yours is the whole project.

3 · Mini-lesson — Dear Reader & the informational essay (~15 min)

  • I'll model two required elements quickly: the Dear Reader (what it does — sets the reader up, something provocative or moving) and the brief informational essay (250–350 words, vivid, straight-ahead, your topic boiled to essentials). See the guidelines.
  • You: pick which one to start.

Purpose: two load-bearing pieces taught fast, so the rest of class is writing.

4 · Work time (~25 min)

  • You: draft the Dear Reader or the informational essay — built from the specifics your sources gave you, not the generic version.
  • I'll circulate.

Purpose: turn research into the first real pages while the sources are fresh.

Wrap-up & before Wednesday:
  • Keep drafting genres, and keep your interview/fieldwork moving.
  • Wednesday: the free-verse poem, the visual element, and your first move from the AI Multimodal Moves List. Draft due Fri Nov 20.
