The Moral of the Story

Week 12 · Monday · Question Lab
My question: When did the semicolon first become popular among writers?
  1. More is not the same as checkable. The plain chatbot named more people. My notebook pointed to pages.
  2. The two tools disagree on facts. For the first printing, the plain chatbot said 1496 and my notebook said 1494. For when it became popular, the plain chatbot said the 1500s and 1600s and my notebook said the 1800s. A disagreement is a lead into the sources.
  3. Each tool finds things the other doesn't.
  4. Agreement on the big things can hide a disagreement in the details. Both named the same book, with different years.
  5. The AI's check can be wrong too. That is why the class goes first and the sources settle it.
  6. Read carefully.
