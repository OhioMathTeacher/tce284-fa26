Ask two tools the same question. Type the class passcode, paste each answer into a box on the left, and press Compare. To compare without AI, click the ▾ beside Compare; the page then lists only concrete details (names, dates, numbers, quotations, cited sources). Then add, edit, or delete items as you talk.
