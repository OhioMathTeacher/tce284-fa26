Week 15 · Monday — Revise for the Particular

Act III · Multimodal Research Project · specific-over-generic · the reflective note · final assembly
Today's goals: Run the revision pass that beats the machine, write your reflective note, and assemble the final for Wednesday.

1 · The specific-over-generic pass (~25 min)

  • I'll model a side-by-side: the machine's generic version of a passage next to a specific one built from a real source.
  • You: run one of your pieces against the machine's version — cut what's generic, keep and sharpen what only your sources hold.

Purpose: the revision move at the heart of the project — trade the average for the particular.

2 · Write the reflective note (~25 min)

  • I'll name what a strong note needs (it's worth as much as the whole's impact): where AI helped, where it flattened your material, and what you changed and why — candid, specific, praise or critique so long as it's earned. See the rubric in the guidelines.
  • You: draft it now, while the whole project is in front of you.

Purpose: the metacognition is graded heavily on purpose — this is where you show you understand the machine.

3 · Final assembly & presentation signup (~25 min)

  • You: order the pieces, label each required element, add an optional table of contents, and sign up for a Wednesday share slot.
  • I'll circulate and hold the signup.

Purpose: a finished, legible whole — and a plan for the share-out.

Wrap-up & before Wednesday:
  • Wednesday: bring your transformed artifact to share — the thing the machine couldn't make. It does not have to be finished.
  • Your Writer's Notebook is due Wednesday, our last class. You write the Look-Back Letter that day, so it is complete when you hand it in.
  • The MRP — final piece, artifact and reflective note — is due Wed Dec 9. You get the week after the share-out to use what you heard.
