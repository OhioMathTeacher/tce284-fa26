What your draft needs

Due Fri Nov 20 at 11:59 p.m. in Canvas.

  • The eight required pieces, each one started, in order:
    1. Preface, introduction, or Dear Reader: how the project came to be and how to read it. (Week 12 Monday)
    2. Brief informational essay, 250 to 350 words: vivid, straight-ahead writing that boils your topic down to essentials. (Week 12 Monday)
    3. Poetry in contemporary free verse. (Week 12 Wednesday)
    4. A visual element you made yourself: a photograph, drawing, collage, map, or design. (Week 12 Wednesday)
    5. Unifying elements: a repetend, repeated images, genres that answer each other, a fragmented narrative that runs through the whole. (Week 13 Monday)
    6. Note page(s): where each piece came from, what is fact, what is invented, what a reader might miss. (Week 13 Monday)
    7. Works Cited: at least one long-form work, one peer-reviewed journal article, and one primary source you gathered yourself. (Week 11 Wednesday)
    8. Finale: the last piece, the one that brings the whole project to a close.
  • A writer's note at the front: what feedback you want, and what you are unsure about.
  • One PDF, with each element labeled.
  • Before you submit: put the draft into Gemini Notebook with your sources and ask where it says something your sources don't support.
