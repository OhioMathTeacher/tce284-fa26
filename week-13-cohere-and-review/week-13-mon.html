Week 13 · Monday — Making It One Thing

Act III · Multimodal Research Project · note page · unifying element · build
Today's goals: Add the two pieces that make a stack of genres cohere — your note page and your unifying element — then build toward Friday's draft.

1 · Mini-lesson — the note page (~15 min)

  • I'll show what a note page does: a parenthetical reference in the text points to a note that reveals a dimension you wouldn't otherwise know — the research or the making-of behind a piece.
  • You: mark which of your pieces need a note.

Purpose: let the reader in on the research and the craft without breaking the pieces themselves.

2 · Mini-lesson — the unifying element / repetend (~15 min)

  • I'll show a few: a repeated image, a repetend, a fragmented narrative that threads through, a detail from one piece exploded in another — with an example from a real multigenre paper.
  • You: choose the unifying element that runs through your project.

Purpose: the connective tissue that makes many genres read as one work rather than a folder.

3 · Work time (~40 min)

  • You: assemble the draft — get all core required elements present and in an order that makes sense. Thread your unifying element.
  • I'll circulate and sign off on unifying elements.

Purpose: one long block to get from pieces to a whole before the draft deadline.

Wrap-up & before Wednesday:
  • Bring a draft you'd read a piece of aloud. Draft due Fri Nov 20 — all core elements + a writer's note.
