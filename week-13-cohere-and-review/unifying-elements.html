Unifying elements

A unifying element runs through the whole project: a repetend, repeated images, genres that answer each other, or a fragmented narrative.

From the project guidelines. The examples are from our sample projects; each title opens the PDF in Canvas.

A repeated image

When Life Hands You Lemons note 2, page 18

Images of a growing plant come back whenever the story shifts. The writer calls them the repetends.

A piece spliced throughout

Calumet Country Club: New Members Packet Endnotes, page 37

A waitress’s “Unofficial Glossary of Terms” is split up and spliced through the whole packet.

A strip on every page

Frozen Mourning note 3, page 12

A timeline of the voyage and the sinking runs along the bottom of pages 3 to 10, while the pieces above it jump around in time.
