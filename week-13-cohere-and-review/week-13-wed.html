Week 13 · Wednesday: Read and Respond

Act III · Multimodal Research Project · ch23 · peer review in threes · revise
Today's goals: Talk through how Romano revises, read each other's drafts closely, and start revising from what your group said. Your draft is due Friday.

1 · Debrief WWM ch23 (~15 min)
  • You read WWM ch23, "Revision Illustrated". Romano kept all eight drafts of the ending of "Milton's Lie" and shows what he changed in each one.
  • With a partner (5 min) Pick two drafts that sit next to each other. Name one change: something cut, added, replaced, or moved. What did that change do for the ending?
  • With the room (10 min) After Draft 2, Romano "felt an imbalance, an incompleteness, but didn't know what to do about it." What did he finally do? And what did the days between drafts do for him?

2 · Peer review in threes (~45 min)
  • Three rounds, one author's draft per round (15 min each):
    • Read and comment (10 min) The two readers look through the draft and mark it up with comments.
    • Talk (5 min) All three of you talk about the draft.
  • Rotate until every draft has had its round.
  • What to look for: Is the specific beating the generic? Does the unifying element connect the pieces? Is any required piece missing or unlabeled?

3 · Work time (~20 min)
  • You: revise your draft from your group's comments, or work on your transformed AI artifact (see the Moves List).
