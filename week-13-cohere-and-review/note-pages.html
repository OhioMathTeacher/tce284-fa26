Note pages

A note page tells your reader where each piece came from, what is fact, what is invented, and what a reader might miss.

From the project guidelines. The examples are from our sample projects; each title opens the PDF in Canvas.

Piece by piece

Growing Up Spanglish page 25

A note page at the end, titled “Note Page.” Each piece gets a short entry under its own title: where it came from and why it is there.

Numbered notes

Frozen Mourning pages 3 and 12

A reference in the text, “(See note 1)” on page 3, sends the reader to the Notes on page 12. Note 1 says where the newspaper piece came from and why it is there.

Up front

The Allosaurs in Phoenix page 4

A handwritten “Explanation” before the pieces. It says which parts are true and which are made up. A K-12 writer did this.
