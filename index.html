This semester you write about your own life. Not a topic assigned to you — your life, across time: what shaped you, where you are now, where you are going. That is your material, and it is the one material a machine cannot generate. It can write a poem about anything. It cannot run your life for you.

The app

Journaler is where you write this term — the timed gush and the One-Pagers, the currere moments, close reading with highlights, and a dated notebook you export as one PDF to hand in.

It runs in your browser and nothing you write is uploaded. That also means clearing your browser data erases it. Use ⤓ Save my work often and keep the file somewhere you own — Drive, Dropbox, a thumb drive. That file is your real backup, and it reopens on any computer.

The arc

Currere is Latin for “to run.” It is the root of curriculum, which means “the course to be run.” Currere turns that noun back into a verb: not the course you are handed, but the running of it.

  • Weeks 1–5 — tiny curreres. Five One-Pagers. Short pieces, each rooted in one moment and reaching across your life.
  • Weeks 6–10 — the currere. You gather the threads into one longer self-study, and we name the method that has been under your hand all along.
  • Weeks 11–15 — the multimodal research project. You open that work into many forms. One life, told many ways.
