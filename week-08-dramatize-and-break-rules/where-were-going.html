What's Todd-in-a-Can?

After Monday's peer review — humans first — paste your draft into the AI studio tool with the Todd-in-a-Can prompt — it's on the First Draft guideline. It reads your draft the way I would — says what's working first, then asks about the one or two things that would help most. It never rewrites a word. Save its review and turn it in with your draft.
